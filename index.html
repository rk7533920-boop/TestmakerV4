Backup & storage

Turn any coaching PDF into questions with AI

  1. Copy the prompt below.
  2. Open Gemini (gemini.google.com or the app). ChatGPT or Claude also work, Gemini handles big PDFs well.
  3. Attach your PDF (question paper, plus its answer key / solutions if separate) and paste the prompt, then send.
  4. Tap Copy on the code block Gemini replies with.
  5. Come back here, paste it in the box, tap Paste text, paste it, and tap Create questions. Check your questions, change Subject & Topic if you like, then Save.

Works for scanned PDFs and any institute's paper. Long paper? Gemini will stop after 30 questions, just reply continue and paste each batch one after another.

Revision method

Manual spaced repetition
Review them on these days

Days are counted from the day you got a question wrong or flagged it, so day 1 is the next day. A question you flagged by hand and never got wrong is reviewed only on the 1st and 3rd review days. Get both right and the flag is removed; get one wrong and it starts the full wrong-question cycle.

Flagged questions

Off by default. While it is off, a flagged question stays flagged until you remove the flag yourself.
